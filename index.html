      Chapter 04 · Neighbors

      Earth is the anchor.
      Everything else is a long way off.

      Now the sky is flipped into distance. We’re at the center. Each ring is ten times farther than the last.

      “Earth-ish” can mean…

        Similar to Earth in a measurement is not the same as Earth-like. We can’t tell what these worlds are like to stand on.

        Chapter 05 · Planet Zoo

        There is no normal planet.

        Our Solar System has no hot Jupiters, no super-Earths and no lava worlds, yet the galaxy is full of them. Each box is a rough group we made up from size, heat and orbit. Every dot is a real planet, colored by temperature and sized by width.

        Groups are our own simple rules, not official categories. Some planets fit more than one; each is counted once, top to bottom.

          Chapter 09 · How Far Is Far

          These numbers are too big to feel.
          So let’s shrink them.

          If the distance from Earth to the Sun were one step, how far would you walk to reach each of these?

          One “step” here is one astronomical unit, the Earth–Sun distance. A light-year is about 63,000 of them. Distances are rounded.
